Q1 — Why do I see only 1 item in the Hot tab?

The Hot tab shows only Earnings M&A Filing and breaking news rated HIGH impact. On a quiet news day (market commentary, technical analysis), most items correctly appear as Noise — LOW impact — and go to the All tab instead.

✅ HIGH impact → Hot tab

Q2 earnings beat / miss
SBV rate decision
M&A announcement
CEO resignation / appointment
Analyst upgrade with raised target

📰 LOW impact → All tab only

VN-Index technical analysis
General market outlook pieces
Liquidity / cash-flow commentary
World Cup / seasonal patterns
General "what to watch" articles
💡
How to see all news: Click All (N) tab to see every item with its signal badge, AI summary, and financial impact indicators. During earnings season (July), the Hot tab will fill with BEAT/MISS signals.

Q3 — VN Earnings Calendar Explained

What is it?

The VN Earnings Calendar shows Vietnam's quarterly KQKD (Kết quả Kinh doanh) reporting seasons. All listed companies on HOSE and HNX must file quarterly financials by law. The calendar tracks four seasons per year and automatically flags which one is currently active.

Reporting deadlines (UBCK / HOSE / HNX rules)

QuarterResults coverDeadlineLarge caps report around
Q1Jan – Mar April 30 April 10–20
Q2Apr – Jun July 31 (small caps: Aug 14) July 10–25
Q3Jul – Sep October 31 October 10–25
Q4Oct – Dec Jan 31 (prelim) / Mar 31 (audited) January 10–25

How to know when a specific stock will release earnings

⚠️
No official earnings date API exists for VN stocks. Unlike the US (SEC 8-K filings with exact dates), Vietnamese companies only commit to the quarterly deadline. The actual date is unpredictable unless you monitor their CBTT disclosures.

Practical ways to track earnings releases:

Watch the Earnings News tab in the Earnings Calendar — the AI digest surfaces KQKD news automatically
The Hot tab shows Earnings items with BEAT ↑ or MISS ↓ badges as they appear
Follow HOSE disclosure portal: hsx.vn → Công bố thông tin → CBTT định kỳ
For VN30 stocks (VCB, FPT, HPG, etc.): they typically publish within the first 2 weeks of the season
Check each stock's investor relations page directly

Season status badges

🟢 Active Now / Peak

Results are being published daily. Check the Hot tab every morning. Deadline is within 30 days.

🔵 In Season

The reporting window has opened but the peak hasn't started. Large caps may publish early.

🟡 Upcoming

More than 30 days until deadline. Prepare your watchlist of companies to track.

✅ Completed

Deadline has passed. All companies should have filed. Focus on guidance revisions now.

What to watch: the surprise formula

Surprise = Actual EPS − Expected EPS

The market reacts to the GAP vs expectation, not the raw number.

  EPS 1.00 expected → EPS 1.20 actual  =  BEAT +20%  →  stock typically +3–8%
  EPS 1.00 expected → EPS 0.90 actual  =  MISS -10%  →  stock typically -3–8%

  Good Q + guidance CUT   → stock often drops (priced-in optimism collapses)
  Average Q + guidance UP → stock often rises (future expectations repriced)

  Sector contagion: if VCB beats, watch TCB / MBB / BID for sympathy moves
